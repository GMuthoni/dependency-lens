Dependency Lens · v0.2

The bottleneck may be one level above the technology.

Compare apparently different approaches by what they have to accomplish, what they depend on, and what evidence would prove or disprove a shared dependency.

1. Define the problem

Start with the outcome everyone is trying to achieve. Do not start with a company or architecture.

2. Add the approaches

These can be technologies, architectures, vendors, clinical approaches, products, or competing solutions.

3. Choose what you want to discover

Cross-case synthesis

Different approaches may share a condition for success.

Why this candidate?

Why it matters

Dependency is not the same thing as bottleneck. A dependency becomes a meaningful bottleneck only if failure, scarcity, maintenance burden, or scaling difficulty at that dependency materially limits the outcome.
